Vietnam Travel Guide — Support

Vietnam Travel Guide is an offline travel guide for first-time visitors to Vietnam: 34 destinations, 92 street food dishes, a holiday calendar, essential Vietnamese phrases with audio, and live weather.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app work offline?
Yes. All guides, food, phrases and the calendar work without internet. Only live weather needs a connection, and the last forecast is kept for offline viewing.

What is "Buy me a coffee"?
It is a small, voluntary tip to support the app's development. It is a one-time purchase and unlocks nothing — the app is fully free with or without it. You can tip as many times as you like.

The weather is not loading. What should I do?
Check your internet connection, then reopen the destination. Weather comes from MET Norway (api.met.no) and needs a connection for the first load.

How do I change the language?
Tap the language button at the top right of any tab to switch between English and Vietnamese.

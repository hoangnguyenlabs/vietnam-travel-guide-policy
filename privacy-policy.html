Privacy Policy — Vietnam Travel Guide

Effective date: November 13, 2026
Developer: Hoang Nguyen (individual developer)

Summary

Vietnam Travel Guide does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising SDK in the app. Everything in the app works offline, except live weather, which is fetched only when you view a destination.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device identifiers, usage data, or any other personal information. We do not use cookies, fingerprinting, or third-party analytics.

Live Weather

When you open a destination, the app requests a weather forecast from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway, api.met.no). The request contains only the latitude and longitude of the destination you are viewing — not your own location. The app never accesses your device location. MET Norway's terms are available at api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService.

Tips (In-App Purchase)

The app offers one optional, voluntary tip ("Buy me a coffee") through Apple In-App Purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details, billing information, or any personal payment data.

External Links

The app contains links to Booking.com hotel search pages, which open in your web browser. Booking.com's own privacy policy applies once you leave the app.
